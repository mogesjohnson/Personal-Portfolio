# Concept — /now

## Directions

1. **Log slip.** Metaphor: two dated notes on one sheet. Palette: the site tokens in `src/app/globals.css`. Type: Geist for the sentences, Geist Mono and IBM Plex Mono for the stamps and the shared bar. Signature: a hairline rules itself under the date, then rests.
2. **Radar room.** Metaphor: a status instrument you turn. Rejected. ideas.md keeps that for later, and this request asked for a short read.
3. **Terminal transcript.** Metaphor: a fake CLI session. Rejected. `/shadow-army` already is that screen, and this page would copy it.

## Chosen

Direction 1. The page has to carry two specific statuses in a few sentences. A sheet with two columns does that. It does not reuse the projects essay (serif headline, numbered sections, traveling note) or the homepage hero.

## Principles

- A status page should be readable in one glance. Play can wait until there is a list to play with.
- Shared chrome stays shared. `DispatchBar` is the external-page header, not a new composition.

## R2 check

- No homepage copy, section names, MJ monogram, diagrams, intro words, or hero layout.
- Palette is the live site palette, recorded as an override in `BRIEF.md`.
- No Newsreader. That serif belongs to `/projects`.
